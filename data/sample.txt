Scope Infotech: Company Overview

Scope Infotech (also known as Scope) is a federal contractor that delivers
IT services and solutions to U.S. federal civilian agencies. Scope works
under contract vehicles including GSA MAS, CIO-SP3 and CIO-SP4, OASIS+, and
the 8(a) STARS III GWAC.

Corporate Processes
Scope's internal corporate processes are aligned with CMMI Level 3,
ISO 9001:2015 (quality management), and ISO 27001:2022 (information security
management).

Customer-Facing Work
Customer-facing work at Scope is unclassified. It is aligned with FISMA,
the CMS Acceptable Risk Safeguards (ARS), HIPAA, NIST SP 800-53 at the
FIPS 199 MODERATE baseline, and NIST SP 800-171.

Development Approach
Scope's customer-facing development processes follow the Scaled Agile
Framework (SAFe).

Accessibility
Documents, code, and user interfaces intended for customer use follow
Section 508 and WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines.
